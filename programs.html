J-1 Internship & Training Programs

ALLPRO helps eligible international hospitality students, recent graduates and professionals pursue structured, paid U.S. training experiences through J-1 Intern and J-1 Trainee programs.

🎓 J-1 Intern Program

Designed primarily for current students and recent graduates who want practical training in the United States that is directly related to their academic field.

View Intern Program

💼 J-1 Trainee Program

Designed for eligible professionals who already have relevant education and/or work experience and want advanced, structured training in the United States.

View Trainee Program

Which Program Is Right for You?

If you are still studying or recently graduated, the Intern route is usually the first program to review. If you have professional hospitality experience, the Trainee route may be a better fit. Final eligibility depends on your education, graduation timing, work experience and sponsor review.
