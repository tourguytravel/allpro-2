J-1 Intern Program

A structured U.S. hospitality internship pathway for eligible international students and recent graduates who want practical experience related to their academic field.

Who the Intern Program Is For

The J-1 Intern category is intended for candidates who are currently enrolled in and pursuing a degree or certificate at a post-secondary academic institution outside the United States, or who graduated from such an institution within the previous 12 months before the program start.

ALLPRO screens your profile first, but final eligibility is determined through the designated J-1 sponsor and applicable U.S. Department of State rules.

Program Purpose

The purpose is not simply to fill a normal job. The experience should be structured training that builds practical skills, professional knowledge and cultural understanding in a field related to your studies.

Typical Hospitality Training Areas

Culinary ArtsKitchen rotations, production, banquets, food safety and luxury service standards.
Food & BeverageRestaurant operations, banquets, service standards and guest experience.
Front OfficeGuest services, reception, reservations and property operations.
Rooms DivisionRooms operations, housekeeping coordination, front office and guest experience.

Program Length

Hospitality internship programs are commonly structured for several months and may extend up to the maximum duration allowed for the applicable category, depending on the approved training plan and sponsor authorization.

What You Can Expect

  • Paid training opportunity when the host position is compensated
  • Structured training plan
  • Host employer interview and approval
  • J-1 sponsor review and documentation
  • Pre-arrival preparation and onboarding
  • Ongoing program monitoring during the U.S. experience

  • Currently studying hospitality, culinary arts, tourism or a related field

  • Available to relocate temporarily to the United States
