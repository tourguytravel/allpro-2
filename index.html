Frequently Asked Questions

Does ALLPRO guarantee a J-1 visa?

No. ALLPRO can help candidates prepare, apply and navigate the process, but eligibility, sponsor approval and visa issuance depend on the designated sponsor, U.S. government requirements and the consular process.

Are the hospitality opportunities paid?

Many hospitality Intern and Trainee opportunities are paid. Compensation, hours, housing and benefits vary by host property and are shown on the individual job listing when available.

Can I apply if I am not sure whether I qualify as an Intern or Trainee?

Yes. Start with the onboarding and eligibility screening. ALLPRO can review your education and work history before host submission.

Can I apply to more than one opportunity?

Yes, when your profile fits multiple open positions. Your candidate dashboard will track each application separately.

What documents should I prepare?

Common documents include a résumé/CV, passport, school transcript or diploma, and employment verification where relevant. Additional documents may be requested based on your program category.
